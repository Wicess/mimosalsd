## Where the dates come from

Pages that carry a real date are listed with it in the sitemap: policies use their
reviewed date, articles and guides their last content edit, state pages their statute
review, lab batches their test date. Nothing is stamped with today just to look fresh —
an invented `lastmod` is ignored once and distrusted afterwards.

96 of 108 URLs carry a real date.
