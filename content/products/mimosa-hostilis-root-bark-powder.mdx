Finely milled Mimosa Hostilis root bark, sourced in Brazil and packed in the United States.
Prized by natural dyers for the deep purple it produces on wool, silk and leather, and for
its unusually high tannin content.

## What it is for

- Natural fabric and leather dyeing
- Soap and cosmetic manufacture
- Craft and pigment work
- Botanical research and taxonomy

## What it is not for

This is a raw botanical material. It is not food, it is not a supplement, and it is not
sold for human consumption.

## Working with it

Milled to a consistent particle size so it disperses evenly in a cold or warm dye bath.
Colour depth varies by fibre, mordant and bath temperature. Store sealed, dry and out of
direct sunlight.
